Horror, Survival. "The Last Light." A secondary collapse seals the main shaft and traps three miners in the deep gallery with a single lamp and one explosive charge: Brynn, the level-headed forewoman who knows the layout; Aldric, the loud, impulsive explosives handler; and Fen, a terrified new hire in his first week. With the way out buried, Brynn leads them deeper into the abandoned tunnels — the only direction that isn't rubble — as the lamp begins to flicker. Then they hear it: a scraping in the dark, something large that moves when they move and pauses when they fall silent. It hunts by sound. Fen panics and shouts, and the sounds accelerate. When Fen stumbles and cries out, the thing takes him — Brynn and Aldric hear it happen but cannot see, and when the lamp steadies he is simply gone. Aldric detonates his last charge to collapse a side tunnel behind them and buy a little time; the blast is deafening, and the lamp goes out for good. In total darkness Brynn feels moving air against her face — a ventilation shaft — and she and Aldric climb blind, hands on the rock, the scraping resuming somewhere below them. They drag themselves out into grey daylight and do not speak. Behind them the shaft exhales cold air, and far down in the dark, something moves. They survived; it did not end. The thing is still there.
