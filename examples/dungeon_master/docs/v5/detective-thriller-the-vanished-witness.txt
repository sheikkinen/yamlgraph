Detective Thriller. "The Vanished Witness." The night before a corporate murder goes to trial, the one witness who can convict — Pell, who carries the ledger that proves Consul Drey ordered the killing — is dragged from the court safe house by hired men, and the building is burned behind them to swallow the evidence. Detective Marren arrives at the charred ruin to find both witness and ledger gone and the case collapsed; with her partner Lydia working the records, she traces the abductors through bribed dock watchmen and forged manifests to a guild warehouse in the harbor district. But Pell, half-mad with fear, will not move: he has guessed the court itself is rotten, and he will only come out if Marren tells him the truth about Magistrate Hagen — the very man who hired her, secretly Drey's co-conspirator and the author of the abduction. Marren chooses honesty over the comfortable lie, and Pell, convinced, slips her a duplicate ledger sewn into his coat lining and agrees to testify. In the courtroom she lays the ledger before the assembled court and names Hagen aloud; the magistrate's mask falls in front of everyone. The court accepts the evidence and Pell's testimony, and the case against Drey proceeds to sentencing: the Consul is exiled, Hagen is stripped of office and barred from the bench, and the guild's long grip on the judiciary is finally broken. A story of a missing witness, a corrupt court, and a detective who wins by refusing to lie.
