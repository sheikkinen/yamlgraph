Quest, Adventure. "The Sunken Crown." The southern lords will not swear fealty to the uncrowned Queen Livia without the Sunken Crown — and Usurper Kael's army will fall on them within the month, so Livia summons Eira, a scribe's apprentice who knows the drowned temple from old manuscripts, and sends the veteran soldier Thane Gault to guard her, though he distrusts book-learning. The two leave the capital at dawn, traveling south along the flooded river road. At a swollen fork their way is barred by Ferryman Ossa, a riverlord who tests every traveler: he demands Eira recite the temple's true name from memory, no manuscript, no notes — Gault reaches for his sword, but Eira stays his hand and answers correctly. Satisfied, Ossa ferries them through the flooded gorge and presses a breathing reed into her hands, warning that the temple lies underwater past the second gate. Inside, Eira dives into black water with the reed for her only air while above her Gault holds off Kael's raiders, who reached the temple by another route; she surfaces at last with the Sunken Crown in her hands as Gault, bloodied but alive, drives the raiders off. The two ride north through enemy country with the crown wrapped in oilcloth against Eira's chest, and at the capital she sets it on Livia's head before the assembled southern lords. The oaths are sworn, the lack is answered, and the kingdom has a legitimate ruler before the usurper can march.
