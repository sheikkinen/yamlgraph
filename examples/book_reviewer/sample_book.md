> A lone courier must carry a sealed warning across a frozen river to a besieged outpost. Keep it small and fast — two short chapters, a handful of beats each.

# Synopsis

In Chapter One, Captain Jarek Cole gives courier Kaelen Vance a sealed brass cylinder containing a written warning. The warning states that General Valerius plans to launch a surprise flank attack on Fort Solitude at dawn. Kaelen must carry this message across the frozen Oakhaven River to Commander Orla Thorne, who is defending the besieged fort. Kaelen starts his crossing on the ice. Halfway across, Lieutenant Marcus, an enemy scout under Valerius, spots Kaelen and fires arrows at him from the opposite bank.

In Chapter Two, Kaelen runs to avoid the arrows, but his weight breaks the thin ice. Kaelen falls into the freezing water. He uses handheld ice claws to pull himself back onto the solid ice, though he loses his food and gear in the water. Kaelen crawls to the northern bank while Marcus continues to shoot at him. Kaelen climbs the rocky path to the gates of Fort Solitude. He delivers the brass cylinder to Commander Orla Thorne. Thorne reads the message and immediately moves her defensive ballistas to the western wall. When Valerius launches the flank attack, Thorne's forces destroy the siege engines and hold the fort.

# Cast

- **Kaelen Vance** — A military courier who must deliver a warning message across a frozen river to Commander Orla Thorne at Fort Solitude.
- **Marcus** — An enemy scout lieutenant who wants to eliminate couriers to ensure General Valerius's surprise flank attack succeeds.
- **Orla Thorne** — Commander of Fort Solitude who must defend the garrison against General Valerius's army.

# Chapter 1: The Frozen Crossing

Captain Jarek Cole pressed the sealed brass cylinder into Kaelen Vance’s hands, his voice grim as he delivered the charge: the cylinder contained a vital warning of General Valerius’s planned dawn attack, and it had to reach Commander Orla Thorne at Fort Solitude before morning. With the message secured deep inside his insulated tunic, Kaelen set out.

The wind-swept banks of the Oakhaven River were silent, save for the bitter cold whistling through the skeletal trees as twilight faded into a deep, freezing dark. Before him lay the treacherous crossing—a vast expanse of slick, black ice. Kaelen took his first tentative steps onto the frozen river, his boots whispering against the treacherous surface. High above the valley, silhouetted against the darkening sky, Commander Orla Thorne walked the southern battlements of Fort Solitude, inspecting her shivering sentries, unaware of the threat drawing near.

But Kaelen was not alone. On the far bank, hidden among the shadows, Lieutenant Marcus caught the subtle shift of movement on the ice. He raised his recurve bow, tracking the courier’s slow, deliberate progress across the open expanse.

The tension snapped in a sudden, breathless instant. Marcus drew the string to his cheek, aimed for the center of the courier's mass, and released. The bowstring cracked against the freezing air, sending a heavy broadhead arrow hissing through the dark. Just as the missile tore toward him, Kaelen’s instincts flared. He dropped instantly into a low crouch, slamming his metal ice claws deep into the frozen surface to drag himself forward. The sudden drop saved his life. The arrow flashed through the space where his chest had been a fraction of a second before, slamming violently into the thick ice right beside his shoulder, the shaft vibrating with lethal force.

Up on the high battlements, the sudden commotion drew Commander Thorne's attention. She raised her brass spyglass, scanning the dim river valley below, and focused on the small, desperate figures locked in a struggle on the ice.

# Chapter 2: The Fort Defended

The freezing winds of the Oakhaven River howl between the rocky banks, where Kaelen Vance crouches on the slick, treacherous ice. On the opposite shore, Lieutenant Marcus notches a broadhead arrow, while high above on the battlements of Fort Solitude, Commander Orla Thorne watches the life-or-death pursuit through her brass spyglass. Thorne orders her archers to lay down covering fire, sending a volley toward the northern bank. As Kaelen sprints forward in a low crouch, Marcus looses his arrow, but the descending barrage from the fort forces the lieutenant to duck. The deflected arrow misses Kaelen's chest and embeds itself deeply into the cracked ice ahead of him.

Kaelen drives his weight forward across the fracturing river, but the surface shatters, dropping him instantly into the freezing water. Marcus looses another arrow, which flies over the gap where Kaelen submerged and embeds in the southern ice shelf. Plunging into the dark current, Kaelen releases his heavy gear bag, letting it sink into the depths, and drives his metal ice claws into the edge of the remaining ice to haul himself upward. From the battlements, Thorne orders a recovery detail to the southern postern gate with ropes to secure the runner, while her archers maintain continuous suppressing fire on the northern bank. The volley forces Marcus to shoot in haste, sending his next arrow wide of Kaelen's exposed shoulders. Free from the immediate threat of incoming fire, Kaelen uses the ice claws to drag his waterlogged, freezing body out of the river and onto the solid southern ice shelf.

Marcus fires once more from behind a northern boulder, the arrow striking the rock face behind Kaelen as the messenger scrambles off the ice and up the steep, rocky path toward Fort Solitude. The recovery detail reaches Kaelen on the trail and pulls him through the postern gate into the safety of the fort.

While Marcus retreats into the northern treeline to report the compromised flank to General Valerius, Kaelen bypasses the recovery detail and ascends the barracks stairs. Thorne descends from the battlements at the same time, meeting him on the stone landing. Kaelen retrieves a sealed brass cylinder from his wet tunic and delivers the warning directly to Thorne. She breaks the wax seal and reads the written intelligence dispatch. As she reads, Kaelen points toward the western wall to signal the direction of the impending flank attack.

Reacting instantly, Thorne shouts the order for the ballista crews on the battlements to pivot and aim their weapons toward the western wall. Kaelen, ignoring his physical exhaustion, follows Thorne up the stone stairs to the western battlements. Once they reach the platform, Thorne orders the defense crews to lock their weapons into the western mounts. Kaelen presses his shoulder against the wooden frame of the primary ballista to help the crew pivot it into place, then slams the heavy iron locking pin into the mount to secure it. Below the fort, Marcus has repositioned into the western treeline, establishing a line of sight. He looses a broadhead arrow that strikes the wooden frame of the ballista near Kaelen’s shoulder, splintering uselessly against the iron reinforcement. Thorne scans the trees and orders the crews to load heavy, iron-tipped bolts.

Kaelen throws his entire remaining body weight onto the primary ballista's heavy winch lever, straining against the massive tension of the rope to draw it back to the trigger mechanism. From the shadows of the western treeline, Marcus looses a second broadhead arrow aimed straight at Kaelen's torso. But just as the shaft whispers through the freezing air, Kaelen drops low, using every ounce of his strength to pull the lever home. The arrow passes harmlessly overhead. Spotting the flash of Marcus's bow in the brush, Thorne points and commands the primary ballista crew to fire. The trigger is released with a deafening, metallic crack. The massive iron-tipped bolt tears through the sky and crashes directly into Marcus's position in the trees, shattering branches and ending the threat. With the flank attack successfully repelled, Fort Solitude is saved.
