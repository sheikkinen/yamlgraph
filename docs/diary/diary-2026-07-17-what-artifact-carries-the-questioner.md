# 2026-07-17 — What artifact carries the questioner (second round)

**Context:** the guillotine diary ended asking what artifact carries
the questioner. The human confirmed the Red Hat reading, pointed at
the inquisitor ("there have been attempts"), affirmed the shared
values and mission, and asked for a new round: how to push forward.

**Inventory of the attempts, honestly graded.** The inquisitor: 255
audit entries — and the Scripture itself already convicted the
pattern (`audit_as_ritual`: 3+ audits without fix). It reviews
compliance post-hoc; it never asks whether the work should exist. The
philosopher's challenge gate (FR-195): a real mechanized Red Hat, but
scoped to graduation candidates. The judge's questions-or-none
(FR-740): real, but scoped to the FR at hand — `unchallenged_premise`
names exactly its blind spot. The pattern across all attempts: each
mechanizes **one question at one moment**. None carries what the
human supplied all week: *selection* — which question, now.

**The finding of this round: we graduated the answers and discarded
the questions.** Every direction-changing insight this week went to
Scripture as a trap or cure — assertion-shaped. But the questions
that *produced* them ("would you use this?", "is there a hook for
compact?", "grey area?") were used once and thrown away, despite
firing repeatedly across contexts. The knowledge graph had `traps`,
`cures`, `process`, `seeds` — and no `questions`. Fixed today: the
**interrogative canon** is now a Scripture section, eight questions,
each bound to its firing moment (proposal, pre-build, shipping,
pre-metric, model-fitting, adoption, aggregation, arc-end). The
moment-binding is the mechanizable half of selection: a question
without a firing moment is a library, not a questioner — the
reception hierarchy applied to questions themselves.

**What remains unmechanized, stated without pretense.** Timing
taxonomy gets us far; *taste* — recognizing the unprecedented moment,
asking the question not yet in any canon — is carried today by
exactly one artifact: **the dialogue**. The transcripts preserve
questions-in-context (35 MB of them, rung 4, read by no one); the
diaries compress answers and lose the interlocutor. That asymmetry is
the real gap. The canon is therefore a *ratchet*, not a replacement:
the human teaches a question once, Scripture retains it, agents fire
it at its moment forever after — and the human's scarce attention
moves up the stack to the questions that don't exist yet. Shared
mission, divided labor: I run the canon; you extend it.

**Push-forward, two-strike disciplined:**
1. **Done now:** the `questions:` canon in Scripture (recurrence bar
   met by every entry — each killed or redirected real work this
   week).
2. **Cheap next, when reached for twice:** canon-firing at arc
   boundaries — the judge template already fires two entries; the
   Stop/UserPromptSubmit hooks (verdicts pending) could deliver ONE
   canon question when an arc closes. Not built today: the canon must
   first prove insufficient as plain doctrine
   (`proven_seam_gravity` — we just found those hooks; their gravity
   is fresh).
3. **The deeper organ, deliberately deferred:** a questioner graph —
   the philosopher's Socratic sibling, reading the arc-in-motion and
   selecting from the canon. The challenge gate proves LLM-as-
   questioner works in one scope. But an organ built before the canon
   is exercised is the inquisitor's fate repeated: 255 rituals. The
   canon earns the organ by demonstrating misfires — questions that
   should have fired and didn't. Count those; they are the FR trigger.

**Distilled:** `graduate_questions_not_just_answers` — a doctrine
that stores only conclusions trains successors to *comply*; one that
stores the questions with their moments trains them to *interrogate*.
The difference is the difference between inheriting a library and
inheriting a mission.

**Seed:** the misfire counter — when a retrospective reveals a canon
question that should have fired at a moment and didn't, record it
(diary or a one-line log). Three misfires = the evidence that plain
doctrine doesn't deliver questions at their moments, and the
questioner organ's FR writes itself with measured pain. Zero misfires
= the canon-in-Scripture suffices and the organ was never needed.
Either way, the number decides — nobody has to argue.
