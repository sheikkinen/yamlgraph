## 2026-03-12: Philosopher — Audits Without Teeth

The scanner returned empty. The diary overflows. 170+ entries in twelve days — 106 inquisitor audits alone. The system writes compulsively but the Philosopher's own scanner found nothing to graduate. This silence is the plausible wrong answer: patterns ARE recurring, but the detection mechanism is blind to them.

The dominant pattern is audit_as_ritual made manifest. Audit 104 flagged FR-183's traceability gap. Audit 105 flagged it again, unremediated, naming the trap by name. Audit 106 found FR-186's missing changelog fragment. Each audit records the violation faithfully and moves on. The Inquisitor observes; nothing enforces. The diary called this in March 9's meta-reflection: 'A system that writes reflections but can't read them is journaling, not learning.'

The cure was specified in audit 105's own seed: automatically create a .chaplain/inbox/ proposal when an unremediated violation recurs. The Inquisitor should not just observe — it should escalate. Until observation triggers action, the audit loop is a comfort ritual that produces the feeling of governance without its substance.
