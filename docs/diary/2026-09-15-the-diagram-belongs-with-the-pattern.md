# The Diagram Belongs With the Pattern

The research synthesis made eight pattern boundaries visible at a glance, but
the canonical documents still required readers to reconstruct those boundaries
from prose and ASCII sketches. The useful artifact existed one repository away
from the place where practitioners look for it.

The trap is treating a research visualization as evidence decoration rather
than part of the reference contract. Once a diagram clarifies authority and
control flow without adding a new claim, leaving it only in research preserves
the insight while hiding it from its consumer.

**Heuristic:** When research produces a stable visual form of a canonical
contract, place one diagram beside the owning explanation and keep speculative
candidate diagrams in research until their patterns are promoted.

**Seed:** Could a documentation check compare the canonical pattern index with
diagram coverage without mistaking every code example for an architecture
diagram?
