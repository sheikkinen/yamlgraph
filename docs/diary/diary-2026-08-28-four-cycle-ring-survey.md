# Diary — 2026-08-28: Four-cycle ring survey — inward, outward, outer ring, remainder

**Method:** four widening analysis passes over the same 3-day window
(2026-08-26..28), each cycle changing the lens, not the corpus:
(1) inward — pipeline/process mechanics; (2) outward — value delivered
and latent; (3) outer ring — deviant-daily and the gitclaw family;
(4) remainder — research/control-plane/hva-weekly-bulletin/
csap-black-box-tests + workspace-nested siblings, then this synthesis.

## Cycle 1 — inward (mechanics)

1. **Seat assignment by failure mode, not capability** — twin proof in
   one week: GPT-5.6 Sol as implementer caused csap's 11-FR scope
   explosion; the identical temperament as reviewer produced 3 verified
   findings on PR #248 (csap captain's logbook 2026-08-28). Corollary
   from the post-mortem: GPT-5.5/5.6 alternation across pipeline stages
   produced zero adversarial effect — model-version diversity is not a
   challenge function; input-closure diversity is.
2. **"Optional" deletes the human consumer class** (FR-892→895): every
   machine-facing stage survived scope pressure; the one human-facing
   stage marked "optional" died first. Cure was not restoration but
   impossibility of omission (required variables, loud preflight).
   Scope cuts trim breadth, never a consumer class.
3. **Recurrence energy fragments across vocabulary aliases** (FR-893):
   1,266 diaries → ~1,700 labels for <200 concepts; top "novel" traps
   were aliases of graduated Scripture (`silent_fallback` 34× under
   four names). The canary gate validated itself by failing correctly
   on first contact. Next mechanism: label canonicalization.
4. **Silence is not evidence of absence** (csap NC-455 onion): absent
   logs cannot distinguish never-created from created-can't-start; a
   one-line hello Job falsified two days of theory. Progress in layered
   deployment = the error message changing.
5. **Boundaries fire earlier than designed** (FR-896): raw
   customer-domain repo names nearly committed in the Phase-0 scope
   table, one phase before the designed Phase-4 redaction gate.
   `the_one_law` in the privacy dimension. Mechanical bonus: map→map
   chaining fans out per-branch without a barrier node — lint seed.

Cycle-1 synthesis: the scarce resource is not model capability but
**context hygiene** (closed inputs, right seat, canonical vocabulary).

  components are already composed into shipping public products (C4).
