# Diary — Five Witnesses, One Phenomenon: How the Site Mapper Escaped

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Context:** Five innovation_matrix runs from "different angles" produced six
product candidates for the cheap-map/code-reduce pattern. The operator's
intended acceptance case — site mapper/summarizer → RAG — surfaced in none
of them, despite fi_domain_crawl ("Crawl .fi domains and produce
sitemap-style overviews") sitting verbatim in my own run-0 grep output.

## The trap, compounded three ways

**framing_enumeration_leak:** I defined the pattern as one-JUDGEMENT-per-call
(classify/score/extract/verify) and pasted that enum into every domain
string. Summarization — a transformation, not a judgement — was excluded
before any tokens were spent. Five runs varying the MARKET while inheriting
the same OPERATION CLASS are not five witnesses; the convergence I measured
was partly the convergence of my own framing with itself
(`are_the_witnesses_one_phenomenon`, exactly).

**recursive FR-890 violation:** the whole research premise was "the brief
author's context is the contamination — escape it with orthogonal personas."
I then authored five briefs, sent each to a SINGLE monolithic call with no
persona diversity and no web grounding, and called the repetition
"different angles." I recommended the actual cure (`scripts/research.sh`,
librarian seat) as "next step" in three consecutive summaries and never
fired it. Knowing the doctrine and citing the doctrine is not applying the
doctrine.

**inventory_by_visibility, new costume:** run 0's map census CONTAINED the
answer. I extracted the fact I was primed for (model pinning economics) and
discarded the task-shape taxonomy. The site mapper didn't need ideating —
it needed READING. `read_raw_output_first` has a corollary: the inventory
you already collected is raw output too.

## The heuristic

> Ideation runs are independent only if they vary the AXES OF THE FRAMING
> (operation class, stakeholder, grounding source), not just its surface
> topic. Before claiming convergence, name what all runs held constant —
> that constant is the blind spot's address.

**Superseded by the operator's sharper form (same day): the hidden canary.**
Open-ended ideation has no error signal — any fluent output looks like
research. Cure: the initiator withholds a canonical known-true answer from
every prompt and checks the artifact for it afterward. Recall of the canary
validates the run; absence falsifies it entirely — a framing that filtered
the textbook answer filtered the unknown ones too. This is the positive
control of lab science imported to ideation, and it is mechanizable: code
can check artifact-contains-canary-class, making it the ideation-domain
resolution of `gate_checks_shape_not_substance`. FR-890's AC-09/D-9 was
this mechanism run exactly once without being named (does the
OS-permissions class surface without operator help?). Today is the second
occurrence — recurrence bar met, proposal filed to .chaplain/inbox.

## What the miss cost vs. what caught it

The miss survived five LLM runs, a frequency-grep verification pass, and a
six-candidate product distillation. It was caught by one sentence from the
operator holding ground truth. Same shape as FR-888 (operator supplied the
unexplored class) — which is the incident this week's FR-890 was built to
prevent. Two occurrences of the same failure in two days, on either side
of building the cure: the recurrence bar for actually USING the research
route is met by its own author.
