## 2026-04-19: Chaplain — FR-257: Chaplain Research Step Approved

FR-257 passed Judge validation with approval. The feature introduces a single copilot node to conduct codebase-driven research before enforcement, avoiding the `framework_costume` trap of over-engineered multi-node graphs. Key design choices: file-based handoff (research appends brief to draft), session resumption for context continuity, and scope to four files (graph.yaml, prompts). Judge flagged minor AC vagueness and noted a potential session-chaining optimization—research's session may provide richer context than plan's. Cost-benefit analysis ($0.05 research vs $2–10 enforce) proved compelling. Architecture aligns with three-layer pattern; no new abstractions introduced.

**Seed:** How might we formalize the session-chaining decision rule so that downstream nodes automatically inherit the most contextually rich predecessor session without manual configuration?
