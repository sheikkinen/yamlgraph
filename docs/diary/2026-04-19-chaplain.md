## 2026-04-19: Chaplain — FR-247: Changelog REQ Cross-Validation Gate

FR-247 proposes a two-phase changelog validation gate balancing mechanical efficiency with semantic rigor. Phase 1 catches ~75% of issues (phantom REQs, single-REQ CAP mismatches) without external dependencies; Phase 2 applies LLM-based cross-checking for complex multi-REQ scenarios, dogfooding YAMLGraph as the enforcement graph itself. The design prioritizes cost-efficiency and graceful degradation (--skip-llm fallback for keyless environments). Judge approved with minor observations: CAP counts are cosmetic, pre-commit behavior should auto-skip LLM with warnings rather than fail, and one redundant AC item. The architecture exemplifies the principle that automation should inherit the doctrine it enforces.

**Seed:** How can we generalize this two-phase (mechanical + semantic) validation pattern to other cross-reference domains beyond changelogs, and what would make the pattern reusable across the codebase?
