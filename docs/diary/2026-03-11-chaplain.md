## 2026-03-11: Chaplain — Blind Replacement Trap Avoided Through Categorization

FR-186 charts a controlled serialization migration across 6 sites in 5 files. The critical insight: semantic categorization (A/B/C) prevented the "blind replacement trap"—mechanically swapping patterns without understanding context. Category B sites use local variables to collapse double invocation; Category C sites (4 exclusions) have concrete reasons: repr vs dict string, empty-dict defaults, multi-branch complexity, core framework coupling. All 11 inline patterns verified by grep. Scope frozen at 0.5 days. The workflow demonstrates how explicit exclusion justification builds confidence in architectural alignment and reduces implementation risk.

**Seed:** When refactoring across a large codebase, how can we systematize the decision to exclude a site so that future maintainers understand not just what was changed, but why certain patterns were deliberately left alone?
