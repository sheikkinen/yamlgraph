# The intake that was mostly a mirror

**Date:** 2026-07-18
**Context:** planner-brief §4 review after the `world now` re-distill —
reflecting on the three world-context open questions and their relation
to yamlgraph.

## The asymmetry

Three "intake" questions, three different flow directions:

- **Routing:** we hold a mechanism-level ANSWER (routing as declared,
  lintable graph structure with logged decisions) to a question the
  ecosystem states at the policy level. Nothing to build until a
  consumer brings a real cost constraint; the answer's shape is already
  doctrine (policy as config, decisions as facts).
- **Sandboxing:** the one genuine gap — no declarative per-tool
  permission surface — and the ecosystem's own conclusion ("Agent
  Security Is a Systems Problem" = boundaries over prompts) is
  `two_strike_split` at industry scale, which pre-shapes the eventual
  feature: permissions in YAML, enforced at the tool boundary.
  Research-first holds; chaplain worktrees stay the first named
  consumer.
- **Evaluation:** the flow REVERSES. Refusal witnesses, raw-prose
  observation gates, provenance-quarantined archives, gold-label
  auditing — the world is asking for what our incident record already
  paid for. The coded-classification pattern doc is an answer to this
  question, not a consumer of it.

## The trap noticed

`world-context.md`'s Open Questions are generated by OUR distill prompt
asking "what should a YAML-first pipeline do?" — the intake is partly a
mirror. An intake question that keeps agreeing with the Scripture may
be validation, or may be the frame reflecting itself. Stability across
distills (two runs, different article sets, same three questions) is
evidence but not proof. Cheap control: occasionally read the raw feeds
without the question-frame and see what questions arise unprompted.

## Heuristic

**Classify intake by flow direction before filing anything: answer
(export our position), gap (gate on consumer), or mirror (audit the
frame).** Only the gap class can ever produce an FR; the answer class
produces docs/positioning; the mirror class produces a prompt audit.

## Seed

Should world-context gain an **export-candidates** section — things the
ecosystem is asking that our record already answers (measurement spine,
junk-drawer caps, boundary repair)? First consumer would be the
philosopher's grounding or public positioning, not a graph. The
consumer test applies to it exactly as it applied to the intake rows —
a section nobody reads is fr-board F7 again.
