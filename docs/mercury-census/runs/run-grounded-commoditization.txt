### 2) Recent developments

The main recent shift is that the market is moving from **single-purpose OCR/scraping tools** to **LLM-native pipelines** that do multiple steps in one pass:

- **Crawlers now output LLM-ready formats** such as markdown, JSON, and structured records instead of raw HTML.
- **Document tools now focus on semantic extraction**, not just OCR.
- **Batch LLM APIs** have made large-scale per-item processing cheaper and operationally easier.
- **Open-source stacks are maturing**, making it feasible to self-host web crawling and document preprocessing.
- Pricing models are converging around:
  - **Per-page** for documents
  - **Credit-based** for crawling/scraping
  - **Token-based** for LLM post-processing
  - **Custom enterprise contracts** for high-volume or regulated use cases

A notable trend is that vendors are increasingly selling not just “scraping” or “OCR,” but **end-to-end ingestion for AI pipelines**: crawl, clean, extract, classify, summarize, and prepare embeddings.
