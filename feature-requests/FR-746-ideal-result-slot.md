## Ideal Result

Every FR states its ideal end state before its solution — the
north-star sentence that this week was supplied exclusively by the
human ("agent knows what's ongoing and when the guillotine comes",
"world now") — and the Proposed Solution is visibly the minimal path
back from it. Solutions stop growing past their ideal.

## Problem

`ideal_result_backwards` entered Scripture's `generative_methods:`
with its moment (FR authoring) but no mechanization. The proven
mechanism for author-moment methods is the template slot: the
first-consumer line and questions-or-none were adopted by the very
next FRs, unforced, four-for-four. IFR is a thinking discipline for
the context-holder — a graph would start cold (the same reason
fr_board stayed LLM-free); the template is the right substrate.

**Prior art:** FR-740 (questions-or-none template edit + Sermon
sentence — the exact shape); the first-consumer front-matter line
(diary: proven-seams-gravity seed, adopted same day). Disposition:
completes the method-embodiment series; nothing rejected nearby.

## Proposed Solution

1. TEMPLATE.md gains `## Ideal Result` immediately above
   `## Proposed Solution`: *"State the ideal end state (one
   paragraph). The Proposed Solution must read as the minimal path
   back from it."*
2. One Sermon sentence in the **Plan** paragraph (obligation must not
   depend on the template — FR-737 F6 precedent).
3. The chaplain plan-stage prompt inherits the section when its
   template does (verify, don't build).
