## Why this record is hand-written

`scripts/research.sh` is the defect. FR-933 exists because the research
sole route cannot complete a single run, so the route cannot produce its
own research evidence. This is the catch-22 the FR-933 judgement named as
condition C-6 and explicitly declined to waive on its own authority.

The operator granted the waiver on 2026-08-30 ("waiver given — we both are
intimately familiar with the issue"). Precedent for the form:
`feature-requests/FR-927-retire-fr902-lane-guard-hooks.md:7` waives the
route and dispositions alternatives inline.

This record is therefore **not** persona output and must not be read as
five independent viewpoints. It is one author's dispositioned alternatives
table, and it inherits that author's blind spots — the same author who, an
hour earlier, cited a filename that did not exist and missed FR-408. The
judgement is the adversarial read, not this file.

## `is_this_a_graph`

**No.** The change is Python retry semantics at the LLM-node execution
boundary. There is no per-item fan-out, no multi-stage LLM pipeline, and no
model call in the fix itself — the fix changes what a model call *receives*
on its second attempt. A graph would be the thing under repair, not the
repair.

## Honest limitation

No independent viewpoint was consulted. The strongest unexamined objection
is that A-1 assumes the model can comply once told — if a persona genuinely
cannot express its finding in 400 characters, feedback retry converts a
fast deterministic failure into a slow deterministic failure plus tokens.
AC-06 exists to bound that: exhaustion must surface, not loop.
